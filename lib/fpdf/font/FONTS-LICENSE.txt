Bundled fonts and their licenses
================================

The *.php / *.z pairs in this folder are FPDF font definitions: the metrics
plus a subset (Windows-1252 characters) of the original font program.

FPDF core fonts (courier*, helvetica*, times*, symbol, zapfdingbats)
  Metrics only. Part of FPDF; see ../license.txt.

  comicneue   Comic Neue Regular (c) The Comic Neue Project Authors

  unifont/archivoblack-regular, unifont/italianno-regular (metrics)
  lato, lobster, opensans, pacifico, poppins (metrics only, font not embedded)

All of these fonts are free to use, embed and redistribute. Their source files
are at https://github.com/google/fonts.
